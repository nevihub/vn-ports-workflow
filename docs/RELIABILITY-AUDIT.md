## Out of scope / not attempted

- Porting a real game; routing real engines; Windows-only/paid tooling in CI.
- A mathematical guarantee that the firewall proves absence of copyrighted content — documented as known limitation (heuristics + corpus + magic sniffing + size/path rules).
- Identity-grade human authentication: `--user-ok`/`--waive-proof` are explicit, recorded, auditable approvals — not spoof-proof identity.
